Estimates from composition and grain size, not measurements. Hardness uses a Hall–Petch fit on binder mean free path, calibrated so that 6% cobalt at 5 µm reproduces the 87.6–88.4 HRA measured on the EVERPADS sheet; mean free path comes from Fullman's relation with contiguity estimated from cobalt volume. Fracture toughness and coercivity are fitted to published WC–Co ranges, with coercivity anchored to the 66–80 Oe on the same sheet. Transverse rupture strength depends heavily on sintering, HIP and carbon control, so it is shown as a band rather than a number. The abrasion index scales with hardness and takes the EVERPADS tip as 100; it indicates direction, not tooth life. Quote certificate values to customers, never these.
