## 1. Hypotheses

**H_UB.**

- Universe A is the φ = 0.2 rigid-disk gas with Maxwell walls.
- 𝓜_{N_c} is the parcel map of §2. It is fixed before any Universe B run.
  - Its only Universe A input is the equilibrium modulus K_T,A (Stage 0).
  - Its other constants are derived (ρ_rel) or are pre-registered choices (e, c_h, the Lucy kernel), with robustness arms for e and c_h.
- Then for N_c ∈ {4, 16, 64}, at matched dimensionless conditions, five statements hold.

| | statement | tested by |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** kinetic inheritance of transport | ν_B = ν_A within ± 10 %; ≥ 80 % of the shear stress is kinetic + collisional; Newtonian at ε_p ≤ 0.075; independent of e ∈ [0.8, 0.95] (± 5 %) and c_h ∈ [2, 4] (± 7 %) | PQ1, PQ8, PQ6a–c |
| **H2** pressure closure | K_B = K_T,A within ± 10 %; S_B(k) = S_RPA(k) within ± 10 % | PQ2, PQ5 |
| **H3** predicted acoustic incompleteness | Γ_self,B = ρc_B²/K_B(k) lies in the band of §4 (PQ3) | PQ3 |
| **H4** equilibrium and conservation | T_kin/T_int within ± 3 %; \|a₂\| ≤ 0.03; momentum to round-off; energy drift O(dt²) and negligible | PQ4, PQ7 |
| **H5** predicted wall defect | the near-wall occupancy stress is the mean field of the measured density (W-MF, A1 §2.4) | wall verdict (§7) |

H1, H2 and H4 are positive predictions. H3 and H5 are predicted failures with predicted magnitudes.

The verdict is split:

- a **bulk verdict** (H1–H4);
- a **wall verdict** (H5, separate);
- the **F7 bridging forecast** (not a verdict).

## 4. Primaries, margins and decision rules

**Decision rule (design §6.1).**

- The seed is the unit of replication.
- The 95 % two-sided t-interval is taken across seeds. For ratios it is taken on the log scale, with Welch–Satterthwaite df, then back-transformed.
- **PASS** iff the CI lies inside the margin; **FAIL** iff it lies entirely outside; **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise.

**Universe A uncertainties (A1 §5).** Universe A references enter with their frozen value, SE and df:

- PQ2: the full SE(K_T,A) in quadrature;
- PQ5: the SE of S_RPA propagated from S_A;
- PQ3: the band built from Δ_A's 95 % limits.

## 6. Bulk verdict and the overall category

**Bulk verdict (design §6.4; `bulkVerdict`).**

| verdict | condition |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | PQ1, PQ2, PQ4, PQ5, PQ6a–c and PQ8 PASS everywhere they are run; PQ3 = P-INC at every N_c; PQ7 holds; no label |
| **PASS-NARROW** | F6 alone |
| **FAIL** | any F-label other than F6 alone, or any primary FAIL |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise, after the single extension |
| **VOID** | F0 |

**Overall category (`overallCategory`).** It is always reported together with every F-label; neither replaces the other.

| category | condition |
|---|---|
| PASS | bulk PASS |
| PARTIAL PASS | PASS-NARROW; or the only failures are in the constitutive pressure closure (F2, T-FAIL, or a PQ2/PQ3/PQ5 FAIL), with H1 intact: PQ1, PQ4 and PQ8 PASS (at N_c ≤ 16, and at 64 unless F6) and PQ6a–c PASS |
| FAIL | F1, F3 or F4, or any H1 primary (PQ1, PQ4, PQ6a–c, PQ8) FAIL |
| VOID | F0 |
| INCONCLUSIVE | otherwise |

A failure of the current pressure closure is therefore not a failure of all possible coarse-graining. A failure of emergent viscosity is.

## 7. F-labels (design §7, with the clarifications in force)

**Precedence:** F0 > F3 > F1 > F2 > T-FAIL > F4 > F6. All triggered labels are reported.

| label | trigger |
|---|---|
| **F0** | PQ7 (c) or (d) FAIL; (e) in an included run; > 10 % of a bulk configuration's examined runs excluded |
| **F3** | PQ8 FAIL; or PQ6b FAIL; or PQ1 FAIL with s_occ ≥ 0.20 (N_c ≤ 16 when F6) |
| **F1** | PQ4 FAIL at any N_c; or PQ1 FAIL while PQ8 PASS, PQ6b not FAIL and PQ6c PASS |
| **F2** | PQ2 FAIL at N_c 4 in both c_h arms; or PQ2 FAIL at 16 (or at 64 unless F6); or PQ5 FAIL with PQ2 PASS; or PQ3 T-FAIL-low |
| **T-FAIL** | PQ3 T-FAIL-high at any N_c |
| **F4** | PQ6c FAIL; or PQ1 or PQ2 FAIL at N_c 4 with the c_h 4 arm PASS; or ψ₆ > 0.3 in SL4/SL16, or an oscillatory ensemble-mean decay in T4a1, T16a1 or T64a1 (mean U < −2 SE inside the fit window) |

## 8. Wall verdict (A1 §2.4; separate, never part of the bulk verdict)

**Gates.** These are mechanical identities, PASS by the CI rule:

| gate | statistic | tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| G-W1 | P_wall / core total normal stress | [0.99, 1.01] |
| G-W2 | n_contact kT_w / P_wall | [0.97, 1.03] |
| G-W3 | near-wall total normal stress / P_wall | [0.98, 1.02] |

**Statistic.** R = (Δ_meas/Ĉ_meas)/(Δ_MF/Ĉ_MF) per seed, with margin [0.90, 1.10]. It does not depend on k_s.

**Verdicts.**

| verdict | condition |
|---|---|
| **VOID** | any gate FAIL, or > 10 % excluded, at W4c2 or W16c2 |
| **F5-impl** | the R CI inside the margin at both N_c 4 and 16 (c_h 2) |
| **F5-phys** | the R CI entirely outside at either |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

The c_h = 4 arm and all report-only items of A1 §2.4 are reported. N_c = 64 is excluded from the wall test (A1 §2.5). Wall transport, slip and heat transfer are deferred to UB-0W.

## 9. Statistics, exclusions, reserves, the extension, blinding

**Exclusions (automatic, decided before any observable; `UB0Selection.checkRun`).** A run is excluded for any of:

- a halt or safety failure;
- a non-finite state;
- PQ7(a) in any phase;
- PQ7(b) over the measured window;
- lost collision events.

Unexplained late contacts are **not** an exclusion; they void (F0).

**Reserves.**

- An excluded run is replaced by the next reserve seed of its group, in seed order.
- > 10 % of a configuration's examined runs excluded ⇒ F0 for that configuration:
  - bulk configurations ⇒ F0 for the bulk verdict;
  - W4c2 or W16c2 ⇒ wall VOID;
  - Couette ⇒ reported.

**Run halves (design §11.5.1).** A static group is NON-STATIONARY if the 95 % CI of the seed-averaged (first − second half) difference excludes zero by more than:

| quantity | threshold |
|---|---|
| P̂ at φ 0.18 / 0.22 | K_T,A/150 |
| T_kin/T_int | 0.01 |
| S at a shell | S_RPA/30 |

The primaries a NON-STATIONARY group feeds are INCONCLUSIVE.

**The single extension (design §11.7).**

- **Eligibility:** a primary INCONCLUSIVE at the first look with its point estimate inside its margin. All eligible primaries are extended in one round.
- Every Universe B configuration entering an eligible primary is doubled from its reserve, after any replacements.
- The request (`extension_request.json`) is written by the analysis and committed before its seeds run.
- **Only the extended primaries** use the doubled data, at **97.5 %**. All other first-look verdicts are final.
- No other additional data are permitted. The wall statistic is not extended.

**Blinding.**

- Universe B observables are first computed by `scripts/ub0-analysis.ts`, after the freeze.
- Stability pilots are blind:
  - `observables: false`;
  - a whitelisted stored record (`UB0Blind`): residual ratios, safety diagnostics and seconds only.
- B0 records no Universe B timestep.

**Multiplicity.** Each primary is its own gate (design §11.7). No further correction is applied.

## 11. Computational protocol

- **Code.** The commit of this document. The judged runner is `scripts/ub0-run.ts --plan ub0 --approved-commit <this commit>`; it refuses a dirty tree or another HEAD.
- **Checkpoints.** Per-run checkpoints are byte-identical on resume (tested for all run kinds at N_c 1 and 4). Results are atomic files; checkpoints, which hold the microstate, are deleted on completion.
- **Code changes during the judged runs.** Allowed only for crash or resume fixes proven byte-identical (design G6). Anything else is a deviation that voids the affected configuration.
- **Timing is not data.** Wall-clock appears only in `timing` and `timings.log`.
- **Cost.** B0 (`results/ub0/b0/b0.json`): 0.139 µs + 36.7 ns · c_h²N_c per parcel-step; G = 3.9 / 20 / 91 (c_h 2).
  - Planned judged seeds: ≈ 86 core-hours at Courant 0.025.
  - Design rule (§12.4): if B0 puts the T64a1 runs above 4 core-hours per seed, they are **postponed, not shrunk**, and the bulk verdict covers N_c ≤ 16 ("64 not run (cost)", distinct from F6).
  - T64a1 ≈ 3.2 core-hours per seed at 0.025: not triggered. At 0.0125 (≈ 6.4) it would be. **[D1]**

## 14. Limitations

- One state point (φ = 0.2) and 2D.
- A mean-field pressure closure: its N_c = 4 error is a known risk (≈ 8 %).
- The kernel shape is inherited and untested.
- Wall transport is untested (UB-0W).
- No thermal occupancy law (UB-1).
- Small ν arms for N_c = 64 (6 seeds, at L 160 only).
